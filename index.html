Support

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before booking. Can't find your answer? Reach out directly.

📍

Where do you operate?

We serve Hamilton, Ancaster, and Stoney Creek. We come directly to your home or workplace — no drop-off needed.

🕐

How long does a detail take?

Silver & Gold packages take 30–60 minutes. Platinum and Diamond packages can take 1.5–3 hours depending on vehicle size and condition.

🏠

Do I need to be home?

Nope! As long as we can access your vehicle and have water access nearby, you don't need to be present. Just leave us the keys.

🌧️

What if it rains?

Interior services can always go ahead rain or shine. For exterior washes, we'll work with you to reschedule at no charge if weather doesn't cooperate.

💳

How does payment work?

You pay securely online through Stripe when you book your package. All major credit and debit cards accepted. Your payment is processed instantly.

❌

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens — we get it. Please give us at least 24 hours notice to reschedule. No-shows or last-minute cancellations may be subject to a fee.

🚙

Do you charge extra for SUVs?

Yes, SUVs, trucks, and larger vehicles may have a small additional charge depending on size and condition. We'll always confirm pricing before starting.

⭐

Do you offer recurring plans?

Yes! Monthly maintenance plans are available for customers who want to keep their vehicle in top shape year-round. Contact us to set one up.

Still have a question?

Reach out directly and we'll get back to you as soon as possible. We're happy to help with anything before you book.
